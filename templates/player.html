How to play

🎯 The goal

Everyone is secretly on the Blue team or the Red team. Two people matter most: the President (Blue) and the Bomber (Red).

  • Blue wins if, when the game ends, the President is NOT in the same room as the Bomber.
  • Red wins if the President IS in the same room as the Bomber (the bomb gets them). 💥
  • Grey characters are neutral and have their own private goal.

🃏 Your card

Tap your card to reveal your secret role, tap again to hide it. Keep it secret! You may show your card privately to someone to build trust — but you never have to be honest, and you should never just hand your phone around.

🚪 Rooms

You're placed in Room A or Room B — this is the real room you should physically stand in. Your screen always shows which room you belong to and who else is in it.

⏱️ Rounds & the timer

The game runs for several timed rounds. The countdown at the top is the same on every phone. Talk, share cards, and figure out who to trust before time runs out.

👑 Leaders

Each round, every room needs one Leader. Tap “I'll be leader” to volunteer — agree among yourselves who it should be (the host can also set it). The leader decides who gets sent to the other room.

🤝 Hostage exchange

When the round timer ends, each room's Leader picks a set number of hostages to send to the other room. Once both leaders lock in, those players physically swap rooms, and the next round begins.

🏁 The end

After the final swap, everyone's location is locked. If the President and Bomber ended up together, Red wins; if they're apart, Blue wins. Your screen shows the result.

💡 Tips

  • If you're the President, decide who to trust with that secret — revealing it can rally Blue, but the Bomber may be listening.
  • If you're the Bomber, blend in and try to end the game in the President's room.
  • You can refresh this page anytime without losing your spot or your card.
